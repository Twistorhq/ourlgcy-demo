# Our LGCY: public demo preview

A static preview of the Our LGCY family history app experience, built for
GitHub Pages. Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No build step, no backend,
no database, no tracking, and no real family data anywhere in this repo.

Every name, date, record, and enrollment detail shown here belongs to
fictional sample families (the Reeds on the story pages, the Shote family
on the home tree), invented for this preview and labeled as sample data
on every page.

## Pages

- `index.html`: the home page is an infinite scroll journey down the
  Shote family line, over a photographic oak. It opens at one sample
  root record (c. 1810) in the canopy; each scroll descends a
  generation, cards and white hairline connectors reveal as they
  arrive, and an era rail on the left edge works as the scroll progress
  spine (1810 to TODAY). Seventy sample people in six generations below
  the founding couple, four branch lines, one focus person with a story
  link, a working "Follow my line" control, and an upload panel at the
  base where the tree keeps growing. The generation sections are
  generated by `tools/build_journey.py`. Hidden in the oak: five
  resident animals and four acorn clusters that respond to hover, tap,
  and keyboard focus (one of them hoots), plus a dragonfly that crosses
  only once per fifteen scroll units, by design. Nothing is labeled;
  the QA note in `qa/QA-NOTE.md` maps every hiding place
- `person.html`: a history book page for a fictional ancestor, Solomon Reed,
  with a life timeline, residence history, occupations by decade, fraternal
  membership, and Dawes enrollment facts, every fact tied to a source chip
- `case.html`: a Connection Case demo, matching a Freedmen side census
  household against a Dawes enrollment card household, signal by signal,
  with an honest confidence state and a "what would prove this" list
- `upload.html`: the upload experience mock. Each person submits their own
  records; the engine sorts, matches, and places them. Files dropped in the
  demo never leave the device
- `reference.html`: the Reference Center mock. "Is this my family member?"
  claims checked one way against the archive, which never reveals its side
  to an unverified asker

## Run it locally

Any static file server works, for example:

    python3 -m http.server 8000

then open http://localhost:8000 in a browser. To regenerate the home
page generation sections after editing the sample data, run:

    python3 tools/build_journey.py

## Design notes

Desktop first, with a full responsive mobile view (the scroll journey
is one-thumb native on a phone). Futuristic without the AI glow: no
neon, no gradient glow, no shimmer; precision, hairlines, frosted
cards, motion, and parallax do the work, in a palette pulled from the
oak itself. Accessibility: semantic landmarks, skip link, labeled
controls, visible focus, keyboard operable animals, uploads, and
navigation; `prefers-reduced-motion` stills the parallax, the reveals,
the animal performances, and skips the dragonfly entirely.

The real Our LGCY app is private and invite only. This repo exists so the
family and reviewers can walk the experience before the full build.
