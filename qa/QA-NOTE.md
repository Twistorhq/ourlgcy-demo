  labeled form controls, aria-current on the active nav item, aria-live on
  the upload file list, SVG tree carries role img with a full text
  description, focus visible styling throughout.

## Manual 15-tell audit (Impeccable, file scan path)

No purple or blue gradients (no gradients at all), no gradient text, no
glassmorphism, no pill badges (source chips and the case state tag are
rectangular with 4px corners, used as data labels), no icon in rounded
square tiles (text glyphs only), no identical card grid (landing uses an
editorial list; content pages use tables, a timeline, and rail boxes), no
cards inside cards, no decorative side stripes (the record cards use a top
rule as a record header, structural), no context free hero stats, no fake
numbered sequences (the upload steps are a real process order), no
decorative animation, no default font stacks (serif display face is a
heritage choice, body is a humanist system stack).

Kept on purpose, with reasons:
- Warm paper background: the surface is meant to read as archival paper
  against a deep pine green and oxblood, with saturated anchors on every
  page. It is a palette decision for a family history product, not a
  default template beige.
- Sample data labels repeat on every page: a hard requirement of the
  ticket (fictional data only), not filler copy.

## Known limitation

This sandbox ships no Chromium or Playwright, so rendered screenshots at
1440 and 375 could not be captured here. Responsiveness is verified from
the stylesheet breakpoints and markup instead. First visual pass on the
live Pages URL is the natural Rosa review step.

## Redesign addendum (Nov 7, 2026, home page rebuilt to the 1E design law)

Justynn rejected the v1 home page and set the law in his own words: less
than a paragraph of copy on the whole page, uploads on the page, and one
huge mother tree rooted at the oldest related record for the Shote
Family, trickling down out wide to present day, each branch carrying a
collection of family records. His reference photo did not reach the
build, so the written spec was treated as the authority. The four
sub-pages are unchanged this pass.

What the new home page is:
- Prose word count, counted by extraction (header nav, demo banner,
  footer, and SVG tree labels excluded per the budget rules): 21 words
  total. The lines are: "One root record. Every Shote since." (6),
  "Add your records" (3), "Drop photos and papers here. In this preview,
  nothing leaves your device." (12). The collection panel adds only a
  two word heading, "Branch records," plus its Close button. Target was
  under 60 words; the page carries about a third of that.
- The tree: one gold root record card at the trunk ("THE SHOTE FAMILY /
  Oldest related record, c. 1810 / Sample record"), a founding couple
  beneath it, then six generations of sample Shote descendants: 4, 8, 14,
  18, and 24 across the widening rows, 70 people in total, 24 present
  day leaves, generation era labels down the left edge (C. 1810 to
  TODAY). Generated by tools/build_tree_svg.py (tidy layout: leaves in
  even slots, parents centered over children), so the fragment in
  index.html is reproducible, not hand placed.
- Branch collections: each of the four generation two branches carries
  a records chip (Ruth 14, Henry 11, Della 9, Moses 12). Chips are
  keyboard operable buttons (Enter or Space), open a panel listing that
  branch's sample collection (record name, year, source label, all
  marked sample), close on the Close button or Escape, and return focus
  to the chip. The panel is plain HTML over the page, a bottom sheet at
  phone widths.
- Uploads: the same on device drop zone mock as the upload page (same
  ids, same app.js handler), set into a gold ruled band at the base of
  the tree stage. It lists file names only and sends nothing.
- Nav on the home page is slimmed (Tree, A Life, The Bridge, Upload,
  Ask; shorter header bar). Sub-page navs and footers are untouched.

Re-verified for the redesign:

- styles.css: the v1 home only rules (hero, tree figure, three column
