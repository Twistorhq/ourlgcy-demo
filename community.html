Who lives here

The neighborhood from above: houses, a church, a store, and the stadium, all in one continuous world, and every person on the street is family. Dress follows documented years only: a figure is drawn for the decade of their young adulthood, figured from the birth year on their tree card, and where a record's year is still probable the figure stays era-neutral rather than dressed from it. Each ancestor is kept at the age they were when they passed, where both years are documented: that age never advances, even as the world year does. The world runs on your hours and your calendar: come at night and most everyone is home; come on a Saturday and the family gathers at the stadium, where a relative whose college attendance is documented wears her own school's colors, purple and gold for San Francisco State. Walk toward a relative and she will stop, turn, and greet you by name.
