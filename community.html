Who lives here

The neighborhood from above: houses, a church, a store, and the stadium, all in one continuous world. When a family adds their tree, every person on these streets is one of their own. Dress follows documented years only: a figure is drawn for the decade of their young adulthood, figured from the birth year on their tree card, and where a record's year is still probable the figure stays era-neutral rather than dressed from it. Each person is kept at the age they were when they passed, where both years are documented: that age never advances, even as the world year does. The world runs on your hours and your calendar: come at night and most everyone is home; come on a Saturday and the family gathers at the stadium. Walk toward a relative and they will stop, turn, and greet you by name.

No one lives here yet: this world fills with your family when you add your tree.
