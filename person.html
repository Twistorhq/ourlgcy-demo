A life, told fully

Every person on a family's tree gets a page of their own: their life in order, the household the records hold them in, their names as the records carry them, and the open questions carried openly, with both candidates named rather than one chosen in silence. This preview begins blank, so this page is waiting for its first person. Yours could be the first name on it.

Begin your tree Add your records

How to read this page: every fact carries its status. Documented means a captured record states it. Probable means family reported, corroborated short of a primary record. Lead means a candidate with evidence and a named decider, never presented as a connection. When the record is silent, the page says so, plainly.

What stands here, once your tree grows

  1. First

    Their life, in order. Birth, households, marriages, migrations, passing: each event a line on this timeline, each line carrying the record that states it and the status that record has earned.

  2. Then

    Their household, as the records hold it. Who they were counted with, where, and when, in the record's own terms, with the tensions between records carried openly instead of smoothed away.

  3. Open

    What the record has not settled. The questions stay on the page, labeled open, with the record that would decide each one named. Nothing is invented to fill a gap.

The first page is yours

A story page is built person by person, from records a family submits about their own people. Nobody else's family stands here, and no sample life is written in to fill the space.
