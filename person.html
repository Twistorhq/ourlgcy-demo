Emma Jean Jackson

Born in 1936, as the family tree gives it, and counted in 1940 in the Newby, Oklahoma household of Chester and Dora Lee Jackson. She married Curtis Franklin Hammond, and her daughter Ava carries the line down to the root of this tree. Who her father was is the open question at the center of this family's research, and this page carries both candidates rather than choosing one.

What the record does not say

Her father's name. Two candidates stand, and this page picks neither. The family account, held at 75 to 80 percent confidence, names Troy Wright, the laborer in the Jackson household, as the father of Emma Jean, her brother Wesley, and her sister Idabel. The household reading names Chester Jackson, Dora's husband and the head of the 1940 household Emma was counted in.

What would fill the gap: Emma Jean's own SS-5, which names her parents as she reported them; Troy Wright's SS-5 as the second test; and the 1940 schedule image, whose relationship column speaks where the index cannot.

Her birth date is also not record-verified yet: the tree says 1936, the index estimates about 1937, and her birth record or SS-5 decides.

Her branch

Emma Jean joins two lines on the tree: her mother Dora Weathers roots the Jackson line, and her husband Curtis Franklin Hammond carries the Hammond line's Creek County generation. Her daughter Ava joins both to the root.
